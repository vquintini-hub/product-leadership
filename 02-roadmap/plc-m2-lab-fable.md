## Rocks

Revised 15 Oct and re-entered in the form: Impact vs Effort scores added (after the lab's reality check), OKR links aligned with the roadmap, Hard No #3 changed from #1 onboarding to #13 Fable for Teams. Onboarding now sits in Plan Carefully, scheduled for Q3.

**Rock #1**
#11 wearables, narrowed to a record of staying well. This quarter, a thin first version: in-app activity plus Apple Health sleep data, with Oura later. Impact 4, Effort 3. Connects to KR3 (still on premium at day 180): it gives people something they would hate to lose once they feel well.

**Rock #2**
#6 AI check-in prompts, reframed as a short weekly check-in for users past day 90. Impact 4, Effort 3. Connects to KR2 (weekly maintenance practice): the light weekly touchpoint that turns maintenance into a habit. Wellbeing language only, reviewed by clinical advisory.

**Rock #3**
#4 notification fix (wrong times, timezone bug), framed as reaching people at the moment in their week when they usually practise. Impact 3, Effort 1. Connects to KR1 (180-day retention). It ships first, because the weekly check-in depends on reminders arriving on time.

## Hard Nos

**Hard No #1**
#2 Daily streak. Impact 1, Effort 3. It works against our strategy: people who are well do not need daily pressure, and a broken streak would feel like failure to users who came to Fable during a hard time.

**Hard No #2**
#3 Social layer. Impact 1, Effort 4. People chose Fable as a private place for a hard stretch. Sharing progress works against that trust and does nothing for retention after day 90.

**Hard No #3**
#13 Fable for Teams. Impact 1, Effort 5. It is a different business with different buyers, a B2B sales motion and no effect on retention after day 90.
