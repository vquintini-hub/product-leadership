Scenario: Fable (B2C), The Growth Wall

## Exercise 1, Step 1: Playing to Win cascade

**0. Winning aspiration**
People who used Fable through a hard time stay steady after day 90. They keep opening Fable each week because it helps them stay well.

**1. Where to play**
Premium subscribers who used Fable regularly through their first 90 days, in the weeks when they start to feel better and skip sessions. Our existing markets. Mobile app, plus wearables such as Oura and Apple Health. Not new-user acquisition, not clinical care, not social features.

**2. How to win**
Headspace and Calm sell more content. Fable sells staying well. We already hold months of each person's practice history from their hard time. Combined with their wearable data, that gives them a view of how well they are that a new app cannot offer on day one.

**3. Capabilities required**
Build: a weekly maintenance practice adapted from the existing library, a private wellbeing view, and light support when someone's patterns change. Partner: Oura and Apple Health for wearable data. Keep close: clinical advisory reviews every support flow, so it stays on the wellbeing side of the line.

**4. Management systems**
Weekly review of retention after day 90, by cohort. Monthly review of maintenance-plan uptake and downgrades from full premium, to watch for cannibalisation. Quarterly safety review with clinical advisory.

## Exercise 1, Step 2: One hard no

**5. Hard no**
We will not treat people who are well the way we treated them when they were struggling, because they no longer need fixing, and daily nudges and crisis-style content would push them away.

## Lab, Step 1: OKRs

**6. Objective**
People who used Fable through a hard time keep it once they are well.

**7. KR1**
180-day retention of premium users who were regular in their first 90 days: from 30% to 45% by 30 June 2027 (baseline assumed, to confirm).

**8. KR2**
Share of those users doing at least one maintenance practice a week in days 90 to 180: from 10% to 35% by 30 June 2027 (baseline assumed, to confirm).

**9. KR3**
Share of those users still on premium at day 180: from 25% to 40% by 30 June 2027 (baseline assumed, to confirm).

## Lab, Step 2: Reflections after the AI pressure test

**10. Which challenge from the AI is most valid, and why?**
The most valid challenge is that people who are well may not feel they need Fable at all. Our data says they leave because their need is resolved, and my strategy assumes staying well is a need people feel. The whole bet rests on that assumption.

**11. What would you change based on the pushback, and what would you defend?**
I would add something people would hate to lose: their record of staying well, built week by week, with weeks steady, sleep trends and the practices that worked for them. That gives them a reason to stay and keep paying. I would defend the hard no on daily nudges, because a daily streak would bring back the pressure they felt when they were struggling.
