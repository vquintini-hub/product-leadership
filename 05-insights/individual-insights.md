# Individual Insights: Strategy Development Process

> Module 6 · Lead the Team That Builds with AI

## Friction points

> The business case. The brief gives no price, no user numbers and no churn rate, so I had to make up every number, label it, and test it. My first versions mixed up the value of one extra paying user with the number of users needed to cover the cost. I worked through it by writing every number next to its calculation, until someone outside the team could check each step.

## Key learnings

> A Hard No is something we refuse to do. I first listed the onboarding redesign as one, then saw it was only a delay, and moved it to Next. Prioritisation frameworks rank items but do not see dependencies, so I now add the sequence myself. And plain language is part of the work: a charter only works if a new product manager can act on it on day one.

## "Aha!" moment

> The stress test of the business case. If the people we keep cancel faster than I assumed, break-even moves from about 5 to about 8 percentage points, and my 6-point kill line would have kept a losing bet alive. Adding a second condition, on monthly cancellations, is when the kill criterion became a decision I could defend in front of a CFO.
