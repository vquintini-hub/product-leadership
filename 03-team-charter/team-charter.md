# Team Charter

Fable (B2C) · Product Leadership Certification, Deliverable 3 · Valeria Quintini · 26 October 2026

Fable here is the course's fictional mental-wellness app. Team of 14 across product, design, engineering, content and clinical advisory, with three PMs.

## The tension this charter resolves

PM 1 owns onboarding, PM 2 owns retention, and PM 3 owns the AI check-in layer, which appears in both PM 1's and PM 2's areas. The Growth team also has a stake. Who owns what, and who decides when they overlap?

## What We Own

**What this team owns**
Retention: keeping people on Fable once they feel better. We own the app experience for paying users. PM 1: the first 90 days. PM 2: life after day 90, including the record of staying well. PM 3: the weekly check-in and its reminders. We are measured on KR1 to KR3.

**Out of scope**
Bringing new users in and turning them into paying users: owned by the Growth team. It matters, and we work with them where our areas meet. Prices: a leadership decision. Clinical care and crisis support: outside what Fable offers.

**Decisions that need a joint call**
If a change touches another PM's area, both agree before it ships. The main case is the weekly check-in (PM 3), which appears in PM 1's and PM 2's areas. Upgrade messages in the app need Growth's agreement too.

## How We Decide

**Who decides feature and scope calls**
Each PM decides alone in their own area. In a shared area, the two PMs decide together. If they cannot agree, I decide, based on what helps people keep Fable once they are well.

**How conflicts inside the team escalate**
If two PMs disagree for more than two days, they come to me together on a call, not on Slack. I decide within one day.

**Who resolves escalations from outside the team**
I handle disagreements with other teams, such as Growth or leadership. I answer within two days and write the decision down.

## Always applies

All wellbeing wording, including every check-in prompt, is reviewed by clinical advisory before it ships.

## Open question

Who decides how many reminders a person gets in a week? PM 2 and PM 3 could each add one, and users would feel pushed. Proposed answer: PM 3 owns the weekly reminder budget, and any other reminder needs a joint call with PM 3.
